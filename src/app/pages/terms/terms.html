Enrolment

A place is confirmed when the term fee is received and we have confirmed the batch in writing. Enrolment is for the named student and the named term; places are not transferable without the studio's agreement.

We keep cohorts at eight students. If a programme is full, we will offer the next available batch or add you to a waiting list.

Fees & payment

Term fees are listed on the programme pages and are payable in full before the first class, unless a payment plan has been agreed in writing. Fees include tuition, studio time, shared materials and ceramics firing as described in the programme notes.

Personal sketchbooks, specialised papers and any tools you choose to keep are not included. We will send a short material list before the term begins.

We review fees once a year. Any change applies to the following term and will be communicated before enrolment opens.

Attendance

Classes begin on time. Please arrive a few minutes early so the studio can settle. Each term includes one complimentary make-up class, subject to availability, if you miss a session. Additional make-ups are at the studio's discretion.

If a class is cancelled by the studio, we will offer a replacement session or a pro-rata credit. We will always tell you as early as possible.

Cancellation & refunds

If you withdraw more than seven days before the term begins, we refund the fee less a small administrative charge. Within seven days of the start, we can usually offer a credit toward a later term rather than a refund, because the seat has been held.

After the first two classes, fees are non-refundable. If you need to stop for medical or compassionate reasons, talk to us — we will look for a fair arrangement.

The Portfolio Atelier is limited and selective. Its deposit is non-refundable once the portfolio review has taken place.

Materials & safety

Some materials — solvents, sharp tools, kilns — require care. Students will be shown how to use equipment safely and are expected to follow studio instructions. Closed shoes are required in the ceramics and print areas.

Please tell us about allergies, sensitivities or access needs before the first class so we can prepare the room and the materials.

Ceramics & firing

Firing is a slow process and occasionally unpredictable. Work can crack, warp or glaze in unexpected ways; this is part of the material, not a failure of the course. We fire at stoneware temperatures and will keep your work safe until it is collected.

Work left uncollected for more than eight weeks after the term ends may be recycled or donated, unless you have arranged otherwise.

Artwork & photography

Students own the work they make. We may photograph classes, work in progress or exhibitions for the studio archive and our website, but only with consent. You can decline, or withdraw consent later, without affecting your place.

If you would like your work excluded from the end-of-term exhibition, tell us — showing is encouraged, never compulsory.

Studio conduct

The studio is a shared room. We expect care for materials, respect for other students and attention during critique. Repeated disruption, unsafe behaviour or disrespect may lead to a conversation and, in rare cases, to a place being withdrawn.

Adults and children share some evening batches. Guardians of younger students should collect them promptly at the end of class.
