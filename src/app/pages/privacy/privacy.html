Overview

Artino Studio Art Academy (“Artino”, “the studio”, “we”) is an art school in Fort Kochi, Kerala. This policy covers this website and the enquiries, admissions and newsletter subscriptions that begin here.

We aim to follow the spirit of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: collect only what we need, use it for a clear purpose, and give you a straightforward way to ask questions or have it removed.

What we collect

When you write to us or enquire about a programme, we may receive:

  • Your name, email address and, if you choose to share it, a phone number.
  • The programme or subject you are interested in.
  • The content of your message, including any details you volunteer about age or experience.
  • The date and time of your enquiry.

If you subscribe to the studio letter, we collect only your email address. We do not use tracking pixels in our emails.

We do not ask for payment details through this website. Fees are arranged directly with the studio by bank transfer or in person.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and suggest a programme, batch or visit time.
  • To manage admissions, attendance and studio communication.
  • To send the monthly studio letter, if you have asked for it.
  • To keep simple records required for running a small school.

We will not add you to a marketing list because you sent an enquiry. The newsletter is opt-in, and every letter includes a way to unsubscribe.

Cookies & analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies or run third-party tracking scripts. It may use your browser's local storage to remember small interface preferences, such as whether you have already seen the opening animation. That information stays on your device.

If we add privacy-respecting analytics in future, this page will be updated before it goes live.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Your details are seen only by the studio team who need them to answer your enquiry or teach your class.

We may use trusted service providers for email or website hosting. They process data on our instructions and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Retention

Enquiries that do not lead to enrolment are kept for up to twelve months, then deleted. Student records are kept for the duration of study and for a reasonable period afterwards for administrative and alumni purposes. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate.
  • Delete information we no longer need.
  • Stop sending you the newsletter.

Write to {{ site.email }} and we will respond within a reasonable time, usually within two weeks. There is no charge for a routine request.

Children

Many of our students are under eighteen. Enquiries for a child should be made by a parent or guardian, and we ask for the guardian's contact details rather than the child's. Photographs of student work or classes are used only with permission, and consent can be withdrawn at any time.
