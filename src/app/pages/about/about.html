The beginning

One table, still.

Fort Kochi · 2014 – 2026

Artino began above a spice warehouse on Bazaar Road with six students, one long table and borrowed chairs. Anaya Menon had returned from Baroda with a painting practice and a question: what would happen if art classes felt less like school and more like a studio?

Twelve years later, the answer is still being worked out in the same room. The chairs are better. There is a kiln, a press and a digital corner. But the table has not grown, and neither has the class size. Eight seats, one faculty member, and enough time to look at what is actually in front of you.

We are an academy in the oldest sense: a place where people gather around a practice. Children learn to see before they learn to render. Teenagers build portfolios that sound like themselves. Adults rediscover the pleasure of making something slowly, with their hands, in company.

“The work gets better when someone looks at it properly. Everything else — the materials, the curriculum, the exhibition — follows from that.”

Anaya Menon Founder & Painting Faculty
