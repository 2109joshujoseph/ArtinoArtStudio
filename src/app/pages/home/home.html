The studio

Not a class. A practice.

Artino began in 2014 with one long table, six borrowed chairs and a belief that art is learned slowly, in company.

We are not an exam factory and we are not a hobby club. We are a working studio where children, teens and adults make real things with real materials, guided by artists who still make their own work. Every term has a beginning, a middle, a critique and a wall.
